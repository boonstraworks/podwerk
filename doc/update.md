# Upgrade from an older version

Make a backup first. Then download the newer version and upload the content
of its `PodcastGenerator/` folder to your website. If your client asks to
overwrite a file, answer yes to all files. Open your website once
afterwards: you may get an upgrade assistant, or the page asks you to
refresh.

This also works for upgrading from Podcast Generator 3.1 or 3.2 to Podwerk.
Delete the old `vendor/` folder before uploading; Podwerk ships its own
libraries.
